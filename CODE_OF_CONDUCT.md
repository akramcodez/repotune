# Code of Conduct

## Preamble

Repotune is an open source project that aims to provide a high-quality toolkit for scanning, fixing, and maintaining open source repositories. As a community-driven project, we are committed to creating a welcoming and inclusive environment for all contributors, users, and maintainers.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make Repotune a positive and respectful community for everyone. We strive to create an environment where people feel encouraged to contribute, share their ideas, and learn from each other.

## Our Standards

* Be respectful and considerate towards others, regardless of their background, experience, or perspective.
* Avoid using language or tone that is discriminatory, derogatory, or hurtful.
* Be open-minded and willing to listen to different opinions and ideas.
* Focus on the issue or topic at hand, rather than making personal attacks or criticisms.
* Be patient and understanding when interacting with others, especially when communicating online.

## Expected Behavior

* Participate in an authentic and active way, sharing your knowledge and expertise with others.
* Report any instances of harassment, bullying, or other unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers.
* Respect the decisions and boundaries set by the project maintainers and other contributors.
* Follow the guidelines and rules set forth in the project's documentation and code of conduct.
* Be mindful of the impact of your words and actions on others, and strive to create a positive and inclusive environment.

## Unacceptable Behavior

* Harassment, bullying, or other forms of abusive behavior towards others.
* Discrimination or derogatory language based on someone's background, experience, or perspective.
* Posting or sharing explicit or disturbing content.
* Spamming or self-promotion that is not relevant to the project or topic at hand.
* Intentionally trying to disrupt or undermine the project or community.

## Consequences

* Failure to comply with this code of conduct may result in removal from the project, temporary or permanent ban from the community, or other consequences deemed necessary by the project maintainers.
* Repeated or severe instances of unacceptable behavior may lead to permanent expulsion from the community.

## Reporting Incidents

If you experience or witness any instances of unacceptable behavior, please report it to SK Akram at skcodewizard786@gmail.com. All reports will be handled confidentially and investigated thoroughly.

## Acknowledgement

This code of conduct is based on the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1. By participating in the Repotune community, you agree to abide by the terms outlined in this code of conduct.
